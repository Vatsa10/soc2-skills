## Purpose

This policy sets the rules for using {{company}} devices, accounts, networks and data.

## Scope

All {{company}} employees, contractors and interns, and all devices (company-owned or personal) used to access {{company}} systems.

## Policy statements

1. Company systems are provided for business use; limited personal use is allowed if it does not interfere with work, violate law or create security risk. [GOV-01 → CC1.1]
2. Users must lock their screen when unattended and screens auto-lock after 5 minutes of inactivity. [AC-04 → CC6.7]
3. Laptops must have full-disk encryption, an endpoint protection agent and automatic OS updates enabled, enforced by device management. [AC-04 → CC6.7]
4. Personal devices may access company email and chat only through enrolled mobile device management or approved web access; they may not store Restricted data. [AC-05 → CC6.6]
5. Users must not share passwords, MFA codes or tokens, nor use a work password on any other service. [AC-01 → CC6.1]
6. Users must not install unapproved software, disable security tools, or connect unmanaged removable media to company devices. [OPS-01 → CC7.2]
7. Users must not store Restricted data on personal accounts or send it through unapproved tools, including consumer generative AI services. [CF-02 → C1.1]
8. Users must report lost or stolen devices, phishing, or suspected incidents to security@ within 1 hour of discovery. [OPS-01 → CC7.2]
9. Company systems are monitored and users have no expectation of privacy when using them. [MON-01 → CC4.1]
10. Every user acknowledges this policy at hire and annually. [GOV-04 → CC2.2]
11. Unlawful, harassing, discriminatory or malicious content may not be created, stored or sent on company systems. [GOV-02 → CC1.3]

## Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| {{owner}} | Owns the policy and approves exceptions. |
| Managers | Make sure team members understand and follow the policy. |
| All users | Comply and report violations. |

## Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy must be requested in writing, state the business reason and compensating controls, and be approved by {{owner}} (and by the CEO for exceptions affecting Restricted data). Each exception expires after at most 12 months and is recorded in the exceptions register.

## Enforcement

Violations are investigated by {{owner}} and People Operations. Confirmed violations may result in access removal, retraining, disciplinary action up to termination, and termination of contractor agreements. Violations that expose customer data are handled under the Incident Response Policy.

## Review

This policy is reviewed on the cadence in the header ({{review_cadence}}), and also after any major incident or material change to systems or scope. The effective date and approver are recorded in version control history. Next review is due before the end of the cadence period following {{effective_date}}.
