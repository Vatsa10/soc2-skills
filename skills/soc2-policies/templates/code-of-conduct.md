## Purpose

This policy sets the ethical standards {{company}} expects from everyone who works for it, and how concerns are raised and handled. A signed acknowledgement of this code is evidence that personnel know what is expected of them.

## Scope

All {{company}} employees, contractors, interns, officers and board members or advisors with access to company systems or data.

## Policy statements

_Numbers and timeframes in these statements are defaults. {{owner}} must confirm {{company}} can meet each one before approving this policy, because an auditor tests against the policy as written. Statements that start with "(if ... in scope)" apply only when that category is in the audit scope._

1. Personnel act honestly and lawfully, protect customer and company information, and follow all {{company}} policies. [GOV-01 → CC1.1]
2. Personnel disclose in writing to {{owner}} any actual or potential conflict of interest (outside employment, financial interest in a vendor or competitor, close personal relationships with a customer or vendor) before acting on the matter, and {{owner}} records the decision. [GOV-01 → CC1.1]
3. Giving or accepting bribes, kickbacks, or gifts above a value of {{gift_limit}} is prohibited, and any gift above that value is reported to {{owner}}. [GOV-01 → CC1.1]
4. Harassment, discrimination, retaliation and threats of violence are prohibited. [GOV-01 → CC1.5]
5. Every person receives this code at hire and acknowledges it in writing (or through the HR or policy tool) within 30 days of start, annually thereafter, and within 30 days after a material change. [GOV-01 → CC1.1] [GOV-07 → CC2.2]
6. Concerns about ethics, security or suspected policy violations can be reported through an internal channel that accepts anonymous reports ({{reporting_channel}}), or directly to the most senior executive or, for matters involving them, to the board or an outside advisor. [GOV-10 → CC2.2]
7. Reports are acknowledged within 5 business days, investigated by someone who is not the subject of the report, and the outcome is recorded in a confidential log kept by {{owner}}. [GOV-10 → CC2.2]
8. Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is prohibited and is itself a violation of this code. [GOV-10 → CC1.1]
9. Violations follow documented disciplinary steps (documented warning, final warning, termination, or termination of contractor agreements), applied consistently regardless of seniority. [GOV-06 → CC1.5]
10. Personnel keep confidential all non-public information about customers, {{company}} and its vendors, during and after their engagement. [CF-05 → CC1.1]
11. Leadership reviews the number and type of concerns reported, and violations handled, at least annually and records the review. [GOV-03 → CC1.2]

## Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy must be requested in writing, state the business reason and compensating controls, and be approved by {{owner}} (and by the most senior executive for exceptions affecting Restricted data). Each exception expires after at most 12 months and is recorded in the exceptions register, a dated list kept by {{owner}} with requester, reason, compensating controls, approver and expiry.

## Enforcement

Violations are investigated by {{owner}} and People Operations (or whoever handles HR). Confirmed violations may result in access removal, retraining, disciplinary action up to termination, and termination of contractor agreements. Violations that expose customer data are handled under the Incident Response Policy.
